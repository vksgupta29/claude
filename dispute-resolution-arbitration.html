Dispute Resolution & Arbitration

Strategic litigation and dispute avoidance before courts, tribunals and arbitral panels.

What we do

Services in this practice

  • Commercial and contractual disputes

    We represent businesses in disputes over contracts, payments and commercial relationships, with early focus on realistic outcomes and cost.

    • Contract and payment disputes
    • Injunctions and urgent interim relief
    • Recovery suits and execution
    • Appeals and revisions

  • Oppression and mismanagement (Sections 241/242)

    Sections 241 and 242 of the Companies Act, 2013 allow members to approach the NCLT where company affairs are conducted in a prejudicial or oppressive manner. We act for petitioners and respondents.

    • Petitions and responses before the NCLT
    • Interim relief and status quo applications
    • Share valuation and exit arrangements
    • Appeals before the NCLAT

  • Domestic and international arbitration

    We advise on drafting arbitration agreements and appear in domestic and international arbitrations, including court proceedings that support or challenge them.

    • Arbitration clauses and dispute mechanisms
    • Institutional and ad hoc arbitrations
    • Interim relief and appointment applications
    • Challenge and enforcement of awards

  • NCLT, NCLAT and tribunal matters

    Company law, insolvency and regulatory disputes are heard by specialised tribunals with their own procedure. We prepare and present matters before them.

    • Company petitions before the NCLT
    • Appeals before the NCLAT
    • Matters before the Securities Appellate Tribunal and debt recovery tribunals
    • Compliance with tribunal procedure and timelines

  • High Court and Supreme Court practice

    We handle writ petitions, appeals and special leave petitions for businesses and individuals affected by regulatory or administrative action.

    • Writ petitions
    • Civil and commercial appeals
    • Special leave petitions
    • Challenges to regulatory orders

  • White-collar crime and investigations

    Investigations demand early, careful legal advice. We help companies and individuals respond to notices and protect their position.

    • Internal investigations
    • Responding to notices and summons
    • Bail and quashing proceedings
    • Trial defence in economic offences
