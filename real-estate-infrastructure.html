Real Estate & Infrastructure

Transactions and compliance for developers, family offices and commercial tenants.

What we do

Services in this practice

  • Property title search and verification

    A property transaction is only as safe as its title. We investigate title and records and report clearly on the risks and how to address them.

    • Title investigation and legal due diligence
    • Search of registers and records
    • Title reports and opinions
    • Remedies for title defects

  • Commercial lease and licence deeds

    We prepare and negotiate leases and licences that clearly allocate rights, costs and exit terms for landlords and tenants.

    • Lease and leave-and-licence deeds
    • Rent, lock-in and renewal terms
    • Registration and stamp duty position
    • Landlord and tenant disputes

  • RERA compliance and litigation

    We advise on project registration, disclosures and buyer disputes under the RERA framework.

    • Project registration and disclosures
    • Buyer and developer agreements
    • Complaints before real estate authorities
    • Appeals and related litigation

  • Development and joint development agreements

    Development projects need clear terms between landowners, developers and financiers. We structure and document those arrangements.

    • Development and joint development agreements
    • Building bye-law and approval compliance
    • Landowner and developer arrangements
    • Project financing documentation
