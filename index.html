About the firm

Rooted in integrity. Driven by practical commercial judgment.

Derived from the Latin amicus (“friend”), our philosophy centres on steadfast, candid counsel. Legal advice cannot sit in a silo. It has to work with regulatory reality, corporate valuations and your operating goals.

The firm is founded and led by Vikas Gupta, Advocate, with experience across commercial transactions, Companies Act mandates, securities regulation and dispute representation before India’s benches and tribunals.

“We advise not just on the execution of the transaction, but on every regulatory and fiduciary implication that follows it.”Vikas Gupta, Founding Partner
