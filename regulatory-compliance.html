Regulatory & Compliance

Inquiries, filings and statutory compliance before the MCA, SEBI and other regulators.

What we do

Services in this practice

General information on the work we do in this area. It is not legal advice.

  • MCA compounding and adjudication

    Defaults under the Companies Act, 2013 can often be addressed through compounding or adjudication. We prepare applications and represent companies and officers before the competent authorities.

    • Compounding applications
    • Adjudication proceedings
    • Show-cause notice responses
    • Filing and compliance remediation

  • FEMA and overseas direct investment (ODI)

    We advise on inbound and outbound investment under the foreign exchange laws, including reporting and approvals.

    • Foreign direct investment structuring and reporting
    • Overseas direct investment compliance
    • External commercial borrowings
    • Compounding of contraventions

  • Digital personal data protection (DPDP)

    The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 sets obligations for organisations that process personal data. We help businesses understand and meet them.

    • Compliance gap assessment
    • Privacy notices and consent processes
    • Processor and vendor agreements
    • Cross-border data transfer terms

  • Securities regulation (SEBI)

    We advise listed companies, promoters and intermediaries on securities regulation and represent them in regulatory proceedings.

    • Disclosure and listing obligation compliance
    • Insider trading and takeover regulation advice
    • Show-cause notice and adjudication responses
    • Intermediary registration and compliance

  • Competition law

    Competition law affects how businesses agree, compete and combine. We advise on filings and on day-to-day compliance.

    • Combination filings with the Competition Commission of India
    • Review of agreements and conduct
    • Compliance programmes and training
    • Representation in inquiries

  • Employment and labour compliance

    We help employers set up sound employment practices and respond when disputes arise.

    • Employment and consultancy contracts
    • Labour law compliance
    • Workplace policies, including prevention of sexual harassment
    • Disciplinary processes and terminations
