What we do

Services in this practice

General information on the work we do in this area. It is not legal advice.

  • Pre-litigation settlement structuring

    Many disputes can be settled sooner and on better terms through structured negotiation. We prepare the position and document the outcome.

    • Settlement strategy and negotiation
    • Notices and pre-litigation steps
    • Settlement agreements
    • Withdrawal and closure of proceedings

  • Shareholder and founder dispute resolution

    Disputes inside a company can threaten the business itself. We look for solutions that protect value, using negotiation and mediation alongside formal remedies.

    • Founder and shareholder exits
    • Valuation and buy-out negotiation
    • Governance deadlock resolution
    • Mediated settlements

  • Enforceable conciliation accords

    A settlement helps only if it can be enforced. We draft and record settlements so that they carry legal force under the applicable framework, including the Mediation Act, 2023.

    • Mediation and conciliation procedure
    • Drafting settlement terms
    • Recording and enforcing settlements
    • Court-referred mediation
